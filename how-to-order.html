Two Ways To Shop

Ready-Made or Made-to-Order

Every order on Indocolectivo starts from one of these two paths.

1. Buy a ready-made design

Go to Shop, or pick a brand under Brands — LeBoujoux or Rhubee. Every design is available in size S to XXXL. Choose your style (T-Shirt, Long Sleeve, or Hoodie), size, and add it to your cart. Production starts the moment your payment clears.

2. Print your own design

Go to Create and upload your own artwork — or start from one of our templates. Position and scale it on the mockup, pick the product and size, and confirm the preview. There's no minimum order: we print a single piece just as easily as fifty.

3. Pay & track

Check out from Cart using bank transfer, e-wallet, or card. Once payment is confirmed, your order enters production within 1–2 business days and ships with a tracking number sent to your My Account and email.

Common Questions

FAQ

Is there a minimum order for custom prints?
No. Our Create studio is built for single-piece orders — print one shirt for yourself, or fifty for an event. Pricing is the same per piece either way.
What sizes are available?
Every product — ready-made or custom — is available in sizes S through XXXL. See the Size Guide on each product page for exact measurements.
Can I change or cancel my order after paying?
Ready-made orders can be changed within 2 hours of payment. Custom print orders move to production almost immediately once you confirm the preview, so changes after that aren't guaranteed — double-check your design and placement before confirming.
Do you ship outside Jakarta?
Yes — we ship nationwide across Indonesia via JNE and J&T, with delivery estimates shown at checkout based on your address.
What if my item arrives damaged or wrong?
Contact us within 3 days of delivery with a photo of the item. Ready-made pieces with a manufacturing issue are replaced free of charge; custom prints are reviewed case by case since each is made to order.
