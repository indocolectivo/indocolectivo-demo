Our Story

Built by makers, for makers.

Indocolectivo started as a shared print shop for a handful of friends running their own small labels out of a garage in South Jakarta. Splitting the cost of a good printer meant everyone could afford better fabric — and better fabric meant better brands.

Today the collective is a home for independent apparel brands like LeBoujoux and Rhubee, plus a public custom print studio so anyone — not just the brands — can design and print a single piece without a minimum order.

What We Stand For

How the collective works

Shared standard, separate voice

Every brand keeps full creative control over its designs — the collective only sets the bar for fabric, print quality, and fit.

Print without minimums

Our custom studio is built around single-piece orders — you don't need to be a brand to get something made just for you.

Local, always

Every garment is cut, printed, and packed in Indonesia by the same small team the collective started with.
